Split-K decode attention

Spread one token's attention over the whole GPU

The stock kernel gives each of the 24 query heads one threadgroup that walks every key. At 64K keys, 24 long serial loops leave most of a 20-core GPU idle.

1 · split the visible keysup to 128 segments of ≥ 256 keys, honoring the sliding window and position mask
2 · share K/V across the GQA group3 query heads per KV head: each K/V row is loaded once, used three times
3 · merge with log-sum-expa second kernel combines segment maxima, sums and partial outputs, then adds the attention sink
